Site analysis — August 2026

The read

A 20,460 SF commercial box on 2.1 acres sitting 0.5 miles east of Gallatin Pike, inside the Myatt Drive industrial/flex corridor. The trade area is the real story here — a blended household income of roughly $81,300 across five ZIPs pulled from Hendersonville, Old Hickory and Goodlettsville. The site itself is the problem: no arterial frontage, FEMA flood zone AE, and the building sits at the very bottom of Slumberland's 20,000–45,000 SF prototype.

What's changing

RiverGate Mall redevelopment — ~$450M, 57 acres Merus is converting the closed mall into a mixed-use district: roughly 700 apartments, 100 townhomes and 80 senior units, plus 130,000+ SF of retail and dining, office and medical, and entertainment. Demolition starts spring 2026 with phased delivery into the early 2030s. Backed by TIF from Metro Nashville and Goodlettsville, plus a land donation for WeGo rapid bus transit.
  • In-N-Out Burger planned near RiverGate; Thorntons under construction.
  • The Chadwick — 180 gated single-family homes and townhomes at 251 Newsbitt Lane.
  • Metro is running complete-streets and BRT planning along the Gallatin Pike corridor.

Near term, the mall's closure removes traffic from the node before the new density arrives. A lease signed today buys into the trough.
